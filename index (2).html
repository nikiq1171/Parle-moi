Trouve tes points faibles

Read 12 short sentences out loud. Each pair targets a sound English speakers often get wrong. The app checks which key words a French recognizer understands and ranks the sounds you need to work on.

Takes about 5 minutes. Speak at a normal pace, as you would in the exam. Don't listen to the model first, it's a test.

Tes résultats

Percentage of key words recognized for each sound. The lowest scores are where to start.

Speech recognition isn't a teacher: a low score is a strong hint, not a verdict. Use it to decide what to drill.

Le rythme français

English stresses one syllable per word (PHO-to-graph). French doesn't. Give every syllable the same length and put a slight stress only on the last syllable of each group of words: je voudrais un café / s'il vous plaît. Flattening your rhythm is one of the fastest ways to sound less anglophone.
